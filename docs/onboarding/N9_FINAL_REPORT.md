## Limitations (plain language)

- The 20-bank list is a release *candidate* running locally on dev infra;
  the four review-only banks will always land REVIEW — by design, not a bug.
- "VERIFIED" means the engine recomputed the printed arithmetic from the
  file's own anchors — it is not a fraud check and not a bank-side
  confirmation.
- Synthetic demo PDFs prove the pipeline works; they are deliberately
  simple — real statements are messier (which is why REVIEW exists).
- The audit's spec-independent heuristics are noisy on banks whose layouts
  print extra numeric columns (sbi, kotak); flags were investigated to
  heuristic artifacts, not engine errors — but a heuristic audit is weaker
  evidence than a spec-aware one.
- Two-user isolation verified on the job-list endpoint only; the N8
  unauthenticated-read findings are orthogonal and still open.
